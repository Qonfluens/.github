Qonfluens is an R&D and consulting company for environmental risk assessment, founded in 2022 in Occitanie, France.
We turn laboratory, in vitro and mesocosm data into predictive models that regulators, industry and research teams can use.

### What we do

- **Regulatory statistics.** Dose-response and species sensitivity distribution (SSD) analyses that follow OECD test guidelines and OECD guidance No. 54.
- **Mechanistic modelling.** Toxicokinetic and toxicodynamic (TK-TD) models, such as GUTS and PBTK, that explain effects over time and extrapolate them to new exposure scenarios.
- **Spatial risk.** Exposure to pollutant mixtures and their effects across landscapes and food webs, from soil contamination maps to risk indices.
